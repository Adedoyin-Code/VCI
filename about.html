ABOUT THE PROGRAMME

An invitation to be part of a lasting legacy

Celebrating 80 years of excellence. Building a lasting legacy. Let us celebrate our legacy. Let us build its future.

Our fundraising objectives

  • Supporting the successful delivery of the 80th Anniversary programme.
  • Supporting identified development and improvement projects of the College.
  • Providing opportunities for alumni, friends and organisations to make meaningful contributions to the future of the College.
  • Establishing sustainable initiatives that will extend the impact of the 80th Anniversary beyond the celebration itself.

80th Anniversary legacy projects

Remodelling and upgrading of the School Hall
Asphalting of the access road from the main gate to the Sports Complex and Library
Rebranding and beautification of school buildings in the school’s official colours

Sponsors and major donors may have the opportunity to support or “adopt” an identified project, subject to the approval of the EXCO.

Legacy giving

The 80th Anniversary Legacy Fund provides an opportunity for distinguished alumni, families, organisations and friends of the College to make substantial contributions towards projects that will continue to benefit the College long after the celebration. Such contributions may be associated with an approved scholarship, facility, academic initiative or prize.

Sponsor recognition
