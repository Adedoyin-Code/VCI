ABOUT US

Eighty years is more than a milestone.

For eight decades, Victory College, Ikare Akoko has shaped lives, nurtured talent and prepared generations for service and leadership. For many of us, it is more than an alma mater. It is part of our story.

As we celebrate 80 years, we invite alumni, friends, corporate organisations and well wishers to join us not only in honouring the past, but in investing in the future of the College.
